Makvin Surgical is a USA-based company specializing in single-use ophthalmic instruments for eye care professionals.
Our product range includes slit, crescent, MVR, and clear corneal knives and blades.
We focus on product quality, reliability, and consistency to support healthcare professionals in ophthalmic care.
